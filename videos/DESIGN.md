# VISION motion identity

## Style Prompt
An authored Arabic technical explainer system built around the approved VISION eye: open angular black outlines with a cyan focus point. A white canvas, monumental Arabic statements, large diagrams, purpose-built fictional software surfaces, and a cyan point that connects information. Confident, precise, warm enough for a business owner. No client footage or content. All numeric examples and interfaces are fictional.

## Reference lock
Primary target: the user-approved VISION logo and approved five/six-scene storyboards. Preserve black/white/cyan, geometric eye, readable independent wordmark. Borrow the hierarchy of work-first agency presentations researched earlier; no copied client screens. The logo is the only brand asset; all illustrations are original SVG/HTML.

## Colors
- #FFFFFF: primary canvas and light foreground on dark panels.
- #080E17: text, strokes and dark panels.
- #00BCEB: focus point, interactive highlights and connection indicators. Never small body text on white.
- #E8F0F3: neutral strokes and muted surfaces.
- #44545F: secondary text on white with readable contrast.

## Typography
Dubai Bold 700/900 for Arabic statements and Dubai Regular for explanations. Consolas for data identifiers only. Arabic letter spacing remains normal. 88px principal headings, 30px explanations, 22px minimum UI labels. Fonts packaged locally for deterministic rendering.

## Motion
1920x1080 at 30fps. Narration-led pacing. Every scene has staggered entrances, 3+ easing types and meaningful demonstrations during the voiceover. Main transition: horizontal masked reveal, 0.55s. Focus iris for key reveals, 0.7s. No content exits before scene transition. Slow finite focus-point/ring motion keeps held demonstrations alive. Ending lands on the full logo and one clear CTA.

## Content constraints
Use approved scripts, including intro sentence: ثم نبرمج لك نظاماً يناسب متطلباتك.
No client names, imagery, websites, records or real products. Mark fictional UI in the persistent footer. Conditional integrations and offline capability must remain conditional. No invented metric, guarantee, testimonial or price. No external TTS inference. No website URL invented.

## What NOT to Do
- No client screenshots or recordings.
- No neon glow, gradients, stock characters, 3D device mockups or decorative card grids.
- No slide deck of static paragraphs. Explain with objects, diagrams and animated steps.
- No crowded text, clipped Arabic, jitter or infinite timelines.
- No stock music dependencies: original subtle synthesized sound bed and action sounds only.
