# VISION educational reels 04–06

Approved scenarios: the request journey (frontend/backend/database), sales versus simplified profit, and undoing a sale. Informative content; no sales CTA. Portrait 1080×1920, 30fps. Narration, licensed catalog music already used in V3, and sound events tied to each meaningful visual action. Narrator Microsoft ar-DZ-IsmaelNeural, fast and intelligible without changing pitch.

## Visual identity
Preserve the VISION angular eye and cyan pupil. White #FFFFFF, ice #EAF7FC, cyan #00BCEB, ink #080E17. Sky #DCEEFF secondary surface. Use black for typography and small structural surfaces; no full dark navy scenes. Dubai Bold monumental Arabic, Dubai Regular body and Consolas for data. No Arabic letter spacing. All demonstration data fictional.

## Layout
Portrait-specific layout, never a landscape video cropped. Main content inside x90–950 and y180–1600; captions around y1690–1790, brand/header at y100. Full-frame changing arrangements, dimensional interface objects and clean explanatory shapes. Headline90–140px, supporting40–54px, labels34px+. Clear contrast and breathing room.

## Motion
Reference reviewed: https://prompt-motion.com/motiondsgnr-9dcfa6 (actual video sampled, research contact sheet ../qa/prompt-motion-reference.jpg). Use its principles of monumental kinetic type, geometric transformation, matched scale travel and a coherent graphic rhythm; do not copy footage. Translate into narrative actions, not arbitrary showreel effects. Other research https://prompt-motion.com/himanshutwtxs-5f4b43 and https://prompt-motion.com/stephanlivera-df17a2.

Entrances0.25–0.5s with varied easing, purposeful overshoot and settle. Continuous camera/object changes, readable dwell. Scene transitions cover the outgoing content without empty fades. Deterministic seekable GSAP timeline. Sync discrete sounds to exact click, snap, count and connection events. No strobing, random particles, loop of floating cards, decorative starbursts, stickers, client screens, copied assets, dense prose or unverifiable business claims.
